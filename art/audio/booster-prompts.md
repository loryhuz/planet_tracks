# Booster sound, ElevenLabs prompts (Sound Effects)

Describe the sound itself, not its use. Generate 3 or 4 takes of each and keep the one that
hits hardest in its first 100 ms (the moment the wheel touches the pad). Save the chosen one as
`art/audio/src/booster.mp3`.

## 1. Main (duration 2 s, prompt influence 0.6)

Sci-fi racing turbo boost: a sharp electric zap and pneumatic burst at the very start, then a powerful jet whoosh that rises quickly in pitch, air rushing past at high speed, a short turbine whine fading out at the end. Punchy, clean, close-up, no music, no voice.

## 2. Arcade (duration 1.5 s, prompt influence 0.7)

Arcade kart speed boost: quick bright synth sweep upward combined with a rocket ignition burst and a whooshing wind gust, energetic and short, ends abruptly. No music, no voice.

## 3. Mars (duration 2 s, prompt influence 0.5)

Booster pad on a desert race track: a deep thump as the wheels hit a pad, a compressed-air blast, then a roaring rising whoosh of wind and sand streaming past a buggy at very high speed, fading out. No music, no voice.
